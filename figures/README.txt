Figures use the harmonized 8,872 pair-day sample across ten German twins, 4 November 2020 to 30 October 2026. All plots are generated from actual stored predictions. No fitted parameters or observations were changed.
Figure 1: equal-twin MAE and percentile intervals from existing 1,944 valid circular calendar-block resamples.
Figure 2: empirical absolute-error CDF with equal weight to each twin; axis displays 0–12 bp, not truncation of underlying data.
Figure 3: opposing spreads both beyond tolerance divided by reference spreads beyond tolerance; each pair-day equally weighted.
